Microscope Components

An optical instrument used to magnify small objects that cannot be seen with the naked eye.

Key Parts & Functions

  • Eyepiece (Ocular Lens): The lens at the top that you look through (usually 10x magnification).
  • Objective Lenses: Primary lenses yielding 4x, 10x, or 40x magnification.
  • Stage & Clips: The flat platform where slides are placed and secured.
  • Coarse/Fine Focus Knobs: Used to adjust the clarity and focus of the specimen image.
